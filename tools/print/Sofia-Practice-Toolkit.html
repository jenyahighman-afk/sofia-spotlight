How to practice at home

The best way to get better between classes: short, often, one thing at a time
For Sofia
and her grown-up

The home session · 35–40 minutes

5 minWarm up. Jumping jacks, arm circles, leg swings, light jog. Always first.
8 minStrong. Hollow hold + superman 3×20s · plank + side plank 2×20–30s · slow relevés 2×15 · one-leg balance 20s · lying leg lifts 2×10.
7 minBendy, after warm-up only. Right, left, middle split + straddle, 20–30s each. A stretch, never pain.
5 minKnees + technique. Pliés in 1st and 2nd facing the mirror 2×8, knees over toes. Slow lunges 2×5 each side. Relevés. Tricks wait until the solo is memorized.
10 minThe dances. Trio: this week's section (page 5). Solo: until Nov 9 from the top as far as you know; from Nov 9 one full-out, eyes up (two back to back on Sundays from Nov 30). Until Nov 29: film one chunk, watch it when you want, try again.
6 minShow in your head from Nov 9 The script on the right.
Mon and Tue are rest days at home. Solo rehearsals are booked as needed. On a rehearsal, class or make-up day, the light week (Nov 23) and the night before the show, do the 15-minute memory run instead. Never 2 days in a row without the solo.

Learn it so it sticks

  1. One chunk. Solo: use Hannah's two videos. Watch one 8-count, pause, do it, then go on to the next 8.
  2. Slow. Do it at half speed 3×, copying arms, feet, head and eyes right from the start. The app practice player can slow the music to 75%.
  3. Music. Do it with the real music.
  4. No help. Turn away from the video and the mirror. Do it alone.
  5. Say it. Say the move names out loud while you mark. Words are a second memory.
  6. Chain it. Add the chunk to the one before it, then run both.
  7. Pause-and-predict. Mom pauses the video anywhere. Sofia says and shows what comes next. When she never has to guess, the step is learned.
  8. From the top. Every session: start at the beginning, go as far as you know, then add ONE new chunk.

Set up a dance spot

  • Clear space, with the mat out for anything upside down
  • Tape an X on the floor for "center front"
  • A sticker on the wall = the judges. Dance to it.
  • Phone on a stand at the side for filming
  • Music ready on the app: the real track for each dance
  • Water bottle, hair up, socks off for floor work
  • Once in December: tape out the stage size for a costume run

Grown-up's job

  • Be the stage crew, not the teacher: music, timer, filming, stickers.
  • Ask, don't correct: "What comes next?" "Where do your eyes go?" "Which fix are you working on?"
  • Keep corrections to what the teachers gave. A teacher's new one goes into practice right away. If something looks off, write it under Questions for my teachers.
  • Video: let Sofia choose when to watch her clips. Every review starts with what went well.
  • Praise the work: "You kept your eyes up the whole time," not "you're so talented."
  • Start at the same time each practice day. A routine beats willpower.

Show in your head from Nov 9

  1. Stand in your opening pose, in your dance clothes.
  2. Play the real song, full speed.
  3. Close your eyes. See the stage, the lights, the judges' table.
  4. Dance it in your head through your own eyes; let your arms and head mark small.
  5. Feel the story. Slow, calm breaths.
  6. Hold the ending until the very last note.
  7. Do it 2–3 times. The last time, watch yourself from the audience.
A grown-up reads the steps out loud for the first two weeks. Picturing it at bedtime is extra and optional: never let it push bedtime later.

Safety rules

  • Warm up before stretching. No oversplits, no forcing.
  • Acro only on the mat or with a spotter. Kick-overs and aerials only in class.
  • If it hurts, stop and tell a grown-up.
  • Stop before tired. Tired teaches the wrong way.
  • Sleep 9–12 hours, same bedtime every night.

My fixes · one at a time · check when the teacher says "yes"

Done on video = it shows on two videos in a row. Then the teacher checks it.
  • Knees over toes in every plié, lunge, landing and floor entry. No knees falling in. (Ms. Brittany, Oct 13)
  • Point + straighten on every floor extension, even as the leg comes down
  • Side leg: pick one. A clean attitude or straight. Never halfway.
  • Eyes up. No mirror, no teacher. Every section has a spot.
  • Long arms. Elbows straight overhead, shoulders down.
  • Passé high. Toe beside the knee, high relevé, sharp spot.
  • Trick prep is dancing. Head stays up until the hands go down.
  • Donut roll: arms down, head ALL the way back. Don't tuck the chin. (Hannah)
  • Hold the ending until 2:00, when the sound is gone.
